Simulated data, documented assumptions

All records are generated using seed 1221. The business, stakeholders, and current process are fictional. Source differences are partly designed into the simulation. No client result, causal effect, or realized improvement is claimed.

One cohort, one denominator

Only leads created at least 30 days before the July 31, 2026 snapshot enter conversion denominators. Stage events must occur within 30 days of lead creation. Related events can occur in a later calendar month. Quotes and bookings are not collected revenue.

Response measures

First response is the earliest recorded First response activity; follow-ups do not reset it. Median response excludes nonresponders, which are reported separately. Thresholds classify historical elapsed hours and are proposed demonstration settings, not agreed service levels.

Limits of the recommendation

Stage gaps do not identify why a lead did not progress. Validate staffing, contracts, contact preferences, and current tools with a real business before recommending implementation. The proposed CRM workflow is documented separately and is not implemented by this dashboard.
