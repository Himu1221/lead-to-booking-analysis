# Data model and metric contract

All records are fictional and generated by `src/generate_data.py` with seed 1221. Monetary amounts, where present, are simulated CAD quote estimates. The analysis does not sum quotes as revenue. Timestamps are consistent local elapsed times in the fictional Regina workflow; business hours are not modeled.

| Table | Grain and keys | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| leads | One originating inquiry; lead_id primary key | created_at; source (Website, Referral, Social media, Phone); service; assigned team (owner) |
| activities | One contact activity; activity_id primary key, lead_id foreign key | occurred_at; activity_type (First response or Follow-up) |
| quotes | At most one quote per lead in this version; quote_id primary key, lead_id unique foreign key | quoted_at; quoted_amount |
| bookings | At most one first booking per lead; booking_id primary key; lead_id and quote_id unique foreign keys | booked_at; quote must belong to the same lead |

Relationships: leads 1→many activities; leads 1→0/1 quotes; quotes 1→0/1 bookings. Future quote revisions or repeat bookings require a revised model; they are outside this project's scope.

## Metric rules
- Snapshot: July 31, 2026 at 23:59:59, fixed for reproducibility.
- Eligible/mature lead: created at least 30 elapsed days before the snapshot.
- Originating cohort: calendar month of lead creation, not month of booking.
- Responded/quoted/booked: first qualifying stage event occurs within 30 elapsed days after lead creation, inclusive.
- Lead-to-booking: booked mature leads / all mature leads in the same filtered cohort.
- Quote-to-booking: booked mature leads / quoted mature leads in that same cohort; blank when denominator is zero.
- First-response time: elapsed hours from lead creation to first recorded First response. Follow-up records do not reset the clock.
- Median response: median first-response hours among mature leads responded within 30 days; excludes nonresponders and reports their count separately.
- Late response: responded mature lead with first-response time strictly above the selected proposed threshold.
- No response: no qualifying First response within the 30-day window; not automatically a lost customer.
- Stage gap: count in one stage minus count in the next within the cohort window; not a diagnosed reason or recoverable revenue.

All cohort filters apply to originating leads; related events may occur in a later calendar month. The funnel aggregates event tables before joining them to the lead table. This prevents multiple activities from multiplying quotes or bookings. No personal customer data, reason-for-loss field, staffing calendar, or marketing-spend table exists.
